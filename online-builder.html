Online APK Builder - Meat Freshness Visualizer

Important: You don't need Android Studio! Use any of these methods to get the app on your phone.

Method 1: AppGyver (Easiest - No Coding)

1 Go to AppGyver: Open AppGyver
2 Sign up for free account
3 Click "Create New App" > "Web App"
4 Import URL: https://meatprofiler.app
5 Click "Build" > "Android" > Download APK

Method 2: Web App (Easiest - No Installation)

1 Open in browser: Open App
2 Tap menu (3 dots) and select "Add to Home screen"
3 App icon appears on your home screen
Works exactly like a native app! All features available including offline mode.

Method 3: Online Build Services

A AppGyver: Try AppGyver

Import your web app and build Android APK online

B PhoneGap Build: Try PhoneGap

Upload your www folder and get APK

C Ionic Appflow: Try Ionic

Connect GitHub and build automatically

Method 3: PhoneGap Build (Simple)

1 Go to PhoneGap: Open PhoneGap
2 Sign up for free account
3 Click "New App" > Upload www folder
4 Click "Build for Android"
5 Download the APK file

Quick Recommendation:

For most users: Use Method 2 (Web App) - it's instant and works perfectly!

For native app: Use Method 1 (AppGyver) - easiest online builder!

Installation Help:

If you get "Install blocked" error:

  • Go to Settings > Apps & notifications > Special access > Install unknown apps
  • Allow your browser/file manager to install APKs
  • Try installing again
